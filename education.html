The Case for Recycling

Why Recycling Matters

Every item you recycle creates a ripple effect that protects the environment, saves resources, and builds stronger communities.

Reduces Environmental Pollution
Recycling keeps plastic and waste out of rivers, oceans, and open land — directly reducing the toxic pollution that harms ecosystems and communities.
Conserves Natural Resources
Recycled materials reduce the need to extract raw materials from the earth — preserving forests, minerals, and water for future generations.
Saves Energy
Manufacturing products from recycled materials uses significantly less energy than producing from virgin raw materials — cutting CO₂ emissions substantially.
Protects Wildlife
Plastic waste kills millions of animals annually. Recycling prevents wildlife from ingesting or becoming entangled in discarded materials in nature.
Reduces Landfill Waste
Landfills produce methane — a greenhouse gas far more potent than CO₂. Diverting recyclables reduces landfill volume and curbs climate-harming emissions.
Supports Sustainable Communities
Recycling creates jobs, reduces municipal waste costs, and fosters cleaner, healthier neighbourhoods — especially in fast-growing Nigerian cities.

Technology

What is a Reverse Vending Machine?

A Reverse Vending Machine (RVM) is an automated recycling device that accepts empty recyclable containers, verifies them, and instantly rewards the user with EcoPoints. Unlike a regular vending machine that dispenses products, an RVM collects them.

Each machine is equipped with AI-powered scanning, secure compaction storage, and real-time wallet integration — operating 24/7 in your nearest mall, university, or public centre.

Item Scanning
Each item is scanned using barcode reading and AI-powered material recognition to confirm it is a valid recyclable.
Material Verification
The RVM identifies the material type — plastic, glass, or aluminium — and confirms it meets recycling standards.
Reward Calculation
Points are calculated based on material type and weight, then credited to the user's wallet in real time.
Recycling Collection
Collected materials are compacted and stored securely until collected by certified recycling partners.

How to Separate Waste Properly

Proper waste segregation at the source is the foundation of effective recycling. Knowing what goes where makes a critical difference.

✅ Recyclable Waste
Clean, dry, and empty materials accepted by RVMs
Plastic bottles (PET/HDPE) — empty and rinsed
Glass bottles and jars — empty and unbroken

Cardboard and clean dry paper

❌ Non-Recyclable Waste
Items that cannot be processed by current RVM systems
Food waste and organic matter
Dirty or contaminated packaging
Mixed or composite materials
Broken glass or sharp materials
Polystyrene foam and plastic bags

Quick Tips

Simple Recycling Tips

Small habits make a big difference. Start with these eight practical tips today.

1
Rinse containers before recycling to avoid contaminating other recyclables in the batch.
2
Remove excess liquid from bottles before depositing them into an EcoReward RVM.
3
Separate recyclables from general household waste at the source using a dedicated bin.
4
Reuse containers where possible before recycling — reduce first, then recycle.
5
Encourage family and neighbours to participate — community impact multiplies quickly.
6
Learn your local recycling guidelines — not all plastics are recyclable in every location.
7
Never litter — every plastic bottle in a waterway takes over 400 years to decompose.
8
Choose products with recyclable or minimal packaging to reduce waste at the source.
