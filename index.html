Our Planet

Why Recycling Matters in Nigeria

3.5M tonnes of plastic waste every year Nigeria generates some of the highest plastic waste volumes in Africa — most ends up in waterways and landfills.
Water bodies at critical risk Plastic pollution clogs drainage systems, leading to floods in cities like Lagos and Ibadan annually.
Recycling saves energy & reduces CO₂ Recycling one tonne of plastic saves up to 5,774 kWh of energy and reduces greenhouse emissions significantly.
Together we build cleaner communities EcoLeey empowers Nigerians to take direct action — earning rewards while protecting the environment.
85%

of Nigeria's plastic waste could be recycled

Yet less than 12% actually gets properly recycled. EcoReward bridges that gap by making recycling financially rewarding, convenient, and community-driven — starting in Abuja, Lagos, and expanding nationwide.
