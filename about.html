Who We Are

Nigeria's First Recycling Reward Platform

EcoLeey was founded on a simple but powerful idea: what if doing good for the environment also rewarded you financially? We built a platform that connects users to Reverse Vending Machines (RVMs) across Nigeria, making recycling as easy, rewarding, and accessible as mobile banking.

Our Reverse Vending Machines are smart devices placed in high-traffic locations — shopping malls, universities, bus parks, and community centers. When you deposit a recyclable item, the machine scans, weighs, and instantly credits EcoPoints to your wallet.

EcoPoints are redeemable as real Naira — withdrawable directly to any Nigerian bank account. We are not just a recycling company; we are a sustainability-focused fintech platform driving environmental change through economic incentives.
